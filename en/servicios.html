Services

Our core is digitalising and organising your company's operations. From there we also handle how it presents itself, where it is hosted, and how it evolves over time.

Digitalisation and automation

The heart of what we do.

We bring to digital what is still handled on paper, in scattered spreadsheets or from memory, and we connect your everyday tools so processes run without manual intervention. What used to depend on one person, and caused delays or errors, now happens automatically and in order. It is the investment with the most direct return: fewer manual hours, fewer errors and less rework.

What is included

  • Digitalising manual and paper-based processes
  • Automating repetitive tasks
  • Online bookings and appointments
  • E-invoicing with Quipu, ready for Verifactu
  • CRM: your customers, organised in one place
  • Automatic WhatsApp and email flows

Web design and development

A fast, solid website that is easy to manage.

We design and build sites that represent your company well and load fast on mobile. Made so customers find what they are looking for, and so your team can update them without depending on a supplier for every change.

What is included

  • Bespoke design, no generic templates
  • Fast, accessible and well positioned
  • Content management included, no third-party dependency
  • Bilingual if you need it (Spanish, English and other languages)

Cloud, migrations and infrastructure

A solid technical base to rely on.

We move your company to the cloud and get the base in order. We migrate your data and email, set up backups and make sure everything is reliable and secure, so the rest of the investment is built on something stable.

What is included

  • Cloud migration (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365)
  • Moving data, files and professional email
  • Automatic backups
  • Connectivity, networking and perimeter security

Ongoing support

We stay with you after handover.

The websites and systems we build evolve with your company. We provide ongoing support to maintain them, improve them and resolve whatever comes up, at a defined pace and in a clear way. A long-term relationship, built so the investment keeps delivering results over time.

What is included

  • Maintenance and backups
  • Planned improvements, agreed with you
  • Direct support when you need it
  • Measurement and adjustments over time

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted individually: scope, timeline and cost depend on what you need, and are agreed before we start. The first conversation is to understand your case, and it is free with no commitment.
