Why OPEX

Every project is scoped to measure, with clear scope and cost agreed before we start.

How we work

The OPEX Method is our own methodology, adapted to each business. We listen, analyse, propose a clear plan, build it in phases and measure the improvement.

  1. 1

    Diagnosis

    We listen and audit the current state of the business.

  2. 2

    Analysis

    We get to the root of each problem and decide what to solve first.

  3. 3

    Tailored plan

    A phased roadmap with clear scope and cost.

  4. 4

    Implementation

    We build in iterations, without disrupting your day-to-day.

  5. 5

    Measure and improve

    We measure before and after and improve in cycles.
