Contact us ES

Digital studio in Barcelona. Precision, focus and results.

We are OPEX. Design and digitalisation with method, rigour and a focus on results.

We help businesses compete in the digital world.

OPEX was founded in Barcelona with a clear goal: to make digitalisation an investment with a return, not a cost. Many businesses still run on manual processes for lack of the right tools and support. We combine process digitalisation, web development and infrastructure so they can operate more efficiently and grow.

We work in a direct way: you always deal with the people doing the work, decisions are quick, and every project is executed with rigour.

Who's behind it

OPEX is the studio of Theo Fernández, with specialist collaborators brought in when a project calls for it. Technical background in Network Systems Administration and, currently, Multiplatform Application Development — with a 7th-place national ranking in JOEL (Java and Python) along the way.

In practice, that means you always deal with the same person, start to finish: whoever designs the solution is who builds it and who supports it afterwards.
