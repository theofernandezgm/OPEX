What working with us is like

What you get at each step, and when you decide.

The journey

Four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Free assessment

    You tell us how your company works and we analyse where time is being lost. No jargon, no commitment.

  2. 2

    Proposal and demo

    We give you a written, phased proposal with scope, timeline and estimated cost. Where the project allows, it includes a demo. Also free.

  3. 3

    Phased delivery

    You approve each phase before it starts. We show you progress and adjust based on your feedback.

  4. 4

    Maintenance by subscription

    After handover, we maintain and improve what we've built. Everything stays in your company's name.

What you can expect

Everything clear, from the start.

Transparency

We explain what we do and why, in words you understand. No black boxes.

Scope and cost in writing

Before each phase you know what it includes, how long it takes and its estimated cost, with a cap that is never exceeded without your approval.

No surprises

If something changes, we discuss it first. The invoice never arrives with surprises.

No pressure

We go at your pace. First we understand, then we propose; you decide when and how.

Our commitment

One accountable lead, from start to finish.

The person who carries out the assessment designs, builds and maintains your project. We reply within 1–2 working days, and every decision is explained in concrete terms.
