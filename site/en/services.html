Services

Our core is digitising, automating and organising your company's operations. From there, we take care of the infrastructure that supports them, the website that connects them to your customers and the support that keeps them up to date. We work in English, Spanish, Catalan and Italian, and help English-speaking owners handle the Spanish side of their operations.

Digitisation and automation

The heart of what we do.

We move what is still handled on paper, in scattered spreadsheets or from memory into digital systems, and connect the tools you use every day so processes run without manual work. What used to depend on one person, and caused delays or errors, now happens automatically and in order. And the result is measured: hours recovered, errors avoided and tasks that no longer depend on a single person.

What is included

  • Digitising manual and paper-based processes
  • Automating repetitive tasks
  • Online bookings and appointments
  • E-invoicing with Quipu, ready for Verifactu
  • CRM: your customers, organised in one place
  • Automatic WhatsApp and email flows
  • AI applied to processes (see below)

Applied AI, with judgement

We use AI when it improves a process, not to decorate it. If a task is better solved without AI, we'll tell you.

What it can do for your company

  • Read and record invoices, delivery notes and orders automatically.
  • Sort emails and WhatsApp messages and draft replies.
  • An internal assistant that answers questions about your company's procedures and documents.
  • Regular reports and summaries, generated automatically.

Our commitments

  • Sensitive actions (sending, paying, recording) require human approval.
  • Your data is handled under GDPR, with named providers, and is not used to train models.
  • If an assistant talks to your customers, it identifies itself as AI.

Infrastructure and cloud

The part nobody sees, and everything depends on.

We migrate your email, files and data to the cloud, set up automatic backups and review your network and devices so everything is stable and secure. It's what every other system needs to run without surprises.

What is included

  • Cloud migration (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365)
  • Moving data, files and professional email
  • Automatic backups
  • Connectivity, networking and perimeter security

Web design and development

A fast, solid website that is easy to manage.

We design and build sites that represent your company well and load fast on mobile. Made so customers find what they are looking for, and so your team can update them without depending on a supplier for every change. And connected to the rest of your systems: forms, bookings and contacts go straight into your CRM or inbox, with nothing copied by hand.

What is included

  • Bespoke design, no generic templates
  • Fast, accessible and built to rank on Google (SEO)
  • Content management included, no third-party dependency
  • Multilingual if you need it: Spanish, Catalan, English and more

Ongoing support

You own it. We maintain it.

Everything we build stays in your company's name: accounts, domains, licences and data. After handover, maintenance, monitoring and improvements are provided through a monthly subscription with a written scope: what it covers, during which hours, and how quickly we respond. If you decide not to continue, we hand over the documentation and all access.

What is included

  • Maintenance and backups
  • Planned improvements, agreed with you
  • Direct support from your project lead, within the agreed hours and response times
  • Measurement and adjustments over time

What does it cost?

Every company is different, so every project is quoted individually. What doesn't change is how we do it:

  1. 1

    Free assessment, proposal and demo

    A session to understand how your company works, a written proposal and, where the project allows, a demo before you decide.

  2. 2

    Phased estimates

    Each phase has a scope, a timeline and an estimated cost with a cap agreed in writing. If anything changes, we discuss it first: the cap is never exceeded without your approval.

  3. 3

    You decide phase by phase

    You can stop at the end of any phase. Everything built up to that point is yours.

  4. 4

    Maintenance by subscription

    After handover, support and improvements continue through a monthly subscription with a defined scope.
