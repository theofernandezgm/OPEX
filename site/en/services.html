What does it cost?

Every company is different, so every project is quoted individually. What doesn't change is how we do it:

  1. 1

    Free assessment, proposal and demo

    A session to understand how your company works, a written proposal and, where the project allows, a demo before you decide.

  2. 2

    Phased estimates

    Each phase has a scope, a timeline and an estimated cost with a cap agreed in writing. If anything changes, we discuss it first: the cap is never exceeded without your approval.

  3. 3

    You decide phase by phase

    You can stop at the end of any phase. Everything built up to that point is yours.

  4. 4

    Maintenance by subscription

    After handover, support and improvements continue through a monthly subscription with a defined scope.
