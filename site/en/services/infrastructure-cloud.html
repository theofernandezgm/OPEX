What we do

The part nobody sees, and everything depends on.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?

Both. We migrate your company's email, files and data to whichever platform best fits the way you work.

Will we have to stop work during the migration?

No. We work in phases and iterations, without disrupting your day-to-day.

Do you set up backups?

Yes. Automatic backups are configured as part of the service, and we review your network and devices so everything is stable and secure.

Do you come to the office?

The assessment can be in person in Barcelona or by video call, whichever you prefer.
