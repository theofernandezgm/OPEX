Digitisation and automation for businesses in Barcelona

We move what is still handled on paper, in scattered spreadsheets or from memory into digital tools, and automate the repetitive work so your team can spend its time on what matters.

What we do

The heart of what we do.

We move what is still handled on paper, in scattered spreadsheets or from memory into digital systems, and connect the tools you use every day so processes run without manual work. What used to depend on one person, and caused delays or errors, now happens automatically and in order. And the result is measured: hours recovered, errors avoided and tasks that no longer depend on a single person.

What is included

  • Digitising manual and paper-based processes
  • Automating repetitive tasks
  • Online bookings and appointments
  • E-invoicing with Quipu, ready for Verifactu
  • CRM: your customers, organised in one place
  • Automatic WhatsApp and email flows
  • AI applied to processes (see below)

Applied AI, with judgement

We use AI when it improves a process, not to decorate it. If a task is better solved without AI, we'll tell you.

What it can do for your company

  • Read and record invoices, delivery notes and orders automatically.
  • Sort emails and WhatsApp messages and draft replies.
  • An internal assistant that answers questions about your company's procedures and documents.
  • Regular reports and summaries, generated automatically.

Our commitments

  • Sensitive actions (sending, paying, recording) require human approval.
  • Your data is handled under GDPR, with named providers, and is not used to train models.
  • If an assistant talks to your customers, it identifies itself as AI.

Frequently asked questions

Which processes can be digitised?

Almost any task that depends on paper, scattered spreadsheets or someone's memory: bookings and appointments, e-invoicing with Quipu ready for Verifactu, customer management in a CRM, and WhatsApp and email messages. In the assessment we find the one that costs you most time and start there.

Do you use artificial intelligence?

Only when it improves a process. If a task is better solved without AI, we'll tell you. Sensitive actions such as sending, paying or recording always need a person to approve them, and your data is handled under the GDPR and never used to train models.

How much does it cost to digitise my company?

It depends on the company, so every project is quoted individually. The assessment, proposal and demo are free. You then receive a phased estimate, each phase with a scope, a timeline and a maximum cost agreed in writing.

Who owns what you build?

Your company does. Accounts, domains, licences and data are in your name from day one.
