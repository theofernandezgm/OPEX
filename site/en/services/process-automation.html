Business process automation in Barcelona

We automate your company's repetitive work by connecting the tools you already use, with n8n, Python or whichever tool fits best, and with AI only where it helps.

What we automate

Invoices, delivery notes and orders

Read and recorded automatically, with nothing typed in.

Email and WhatsApp

Messages sorted and draft replies prepared.

Customers and CRM

Whatever comes in through the website, email or bookings lands in your CRM by itself.

E-invoicing

Invoices with Quipu, ready for Verifactu, connected to the rest of your processes.

Reports

Regular reports and summaries, generated automatically.

Data between programs

What is copied by hand from one program to another today, kept in sync automatically.

Which tools

We work mainly with n8n, which connects hundreds of applications and can run on a server your company owns, and with Python, for anything that needs custom logic. If it fits better with what you already use, we work with Make, Zapier, Power Automate or other tools. We choose the tool for the job, not the other way round.

Our commitments

  • Sensitive actions such as sending, paying or recording need a person to approve them.
  • Your data is handled under the GDPR, with named providers, and never used to train models.
  • If an assistant talks to your customers, it says it is AI.
  • Accounts, workflows and data stay in your company's name.

Frequently asked questions

Which tools do you use for automation?

Mainly n8n and Python. If it fits better with what you already use, we work with Make, Zapier, Power Automate or other tools. We choose the tool for the job, not the other way round.

Do I have to change the software I already use?

Not necessarily. We connect the tools you already use. If one doesn't fit, or an improvement isn't worth it, we tell you before quoting it.

Do you use artificial intelligence?

Only when it improves the process. Sensitive actions such as sending, paying or recording always need a person to approve them, and your data is handled under the GDPR and never used to train models.

How much does it cost to automate a process?

Every project is quoted individually. The assessment, proposal and demo are free; you then receive a phased estimate, each phase with a scope, a timeline and a maximum cost agreed in writing.

Who owns the automations?

Your company does. Accounts, workflows and data are in your name, and if you decide not to continue we hand over the documentation and all access.
