IT support and maintenance for businesses in Barcelona

What we do

You own it. We maintain it.

Everything we build stays in your company's name: accounts, domains, licences and data. After handover, maintenance, monitoring and improvements are provided through a monthly subscription with a written scope: what it covers, during which hours, and how quickly we respond. If you decide not to continue, we hand over the documentation and all access.

What is included

  • Maintenance and backups
  • Planned improvements, agreed with you
  • Direct support from your project lead, within the agreed hours and response times
  • Measurement and adjustments over time

How we start

Every project starts with a free assessment: we understand how your company runs today and give you a written, phased proposal with scope, timeline and a maximum cost agreed in advance. You decide phase by phase.

Frequently asked questions

What does the support subscription cover?

Maintenance and backups, improvements planned and agreed with you, direct support and measurement over time. Scope, hours and response time are defined in writing.

Who do I talk to when something goes wrong?

The lead on your project, within the agreed hours and response time.

Can I cancel?

Yes. If you decide not to continue, we hand over the documentation and all access. Everything stays in your company's name.
